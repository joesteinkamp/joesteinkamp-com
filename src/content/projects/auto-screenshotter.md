Capturing screenshots of a web app by hand is tedious enough that it usually
doesn't happen. This extension automates the crawl — visits every reachable
route and snaps a full-page screenshot — so you end up with a complete visual
inventory of the product without any manual effort.

Built as feed material for design knowledge bases and AI analysis pipelines
where you want to reason over a full product's UI, not just the pages you
happened to remember to screenshot.
