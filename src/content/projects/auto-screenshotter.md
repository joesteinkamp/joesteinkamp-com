Automated capture across an entire app, not just a single page. Auto Screenshotter walks a web app and saves full-page screenshots with minimal setup — useful for generating a visual inventory before a redesign or stuffing context into an AI workflow without doing it by hand.

Part of the broader Chrome tooling work alongside Chromo.
