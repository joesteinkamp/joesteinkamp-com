Capturing a full app for design review, documentation, or inspiration usually means
clicking through dozens of screens one at a time. Auto Screenshotter removes that step:
give it a starting URL and it crawls the app, capturing every reachable state automatically.

Companion piece to the broader design-tooling work — specifically, building the raw
material for design inspection without the manual overhead.
