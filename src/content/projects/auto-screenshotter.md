Screenshots a complete web application by automating navigation and capturing each
view. The use case: generating a full-fidelity inventory of a live product for
design review, audit, or inspiration — without sitting there and clicking through
every screen by hand.

Built as a companion to the design workflow: load it, point it at an app, get a
screenshot set.
