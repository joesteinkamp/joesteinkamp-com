What if interactions were first-class prompts instead of events? Natural Language GUI is a component library that intercepts user actions — clicks, form submissions, selections — and surfaces them as structured natural language, making the bridge between UI and AI model explicit by design.

A design engineering experiment in what "AI-native interface" actually means at the component level.
